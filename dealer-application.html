5. Dealer Rules, Inclusions & Deadlines

The following rules and inclusions are reproduced from the official 2027 dealer application. Please review them before submitting.

What's included with each table

  • Corner: Two 8-foot tables, one 6-foot backup table, four showcases, two lamps and three badges.
  • Single (straight): One 8-foot table, one 6-foot backup table, two showcases, one lamp and two badges.
  • Budget: One 8-foot table, one 6-foot backup table, two showcases, one lamp and two badges.
  • Sponsor Center: The same table-inclusion and badge rules apply.
  • Dealer Trading Room (DTR): Tables are 6 feet long. A maximum of two dealers behind each DTR table is permitted.

Rules for regular dealer tables

  • Straight / Single tables: Owner plus one eligible W-2 employee, immediate family member or registered shared dealer behind the table.
  • Corner tables: Owner plus two eligible W-2 employees, immediate family members or registered shared dealers behind the table.
  • Budget tables: Owner plus one eligible W-2 employee, immediate family member or registered shared dealer behind the table.
  • Sponsor Center tables: The same staffing and badge rules apply to the corresponding straight or corner table.
  • Extra eligible employee badges: $100 each, subject to eligibility and approval. An extra badge does not automatically increase the number of people permitted behind a table.

Badge eligibility

Badges are for W-2 employees, immediate family members and registered shared dealers only. Others must purchase Equal Access or Early Access passes.

Shared dealers

A shared dealer listing adds another dealer or business sign at your table and a separate program listing. The fee is $250 for a single or $350 for a corner.

Important deadlines

  • October 1, 2027: Program print deadline. Later changes will not appear in the printed program.
  • October 10, 2027: Final badge-change deadline. Later access requires a paid access pass.
