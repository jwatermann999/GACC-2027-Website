Published rate is currently $20 for up to 24 hours, with no in-and-out privileges. Venue rates may change by 2027.

Getting to the show

An enclosed skybridge connects the convention center to the William Street garage and nearby hotels. Follow venue signs to the show entrance.

Rideshare & drop-off: Main circular drive at 5555 N. River Road.

Accessible entry & assistance

Accessible parking and elevators are available. Follow posted venue signage for accessible routes. For wheelchair requests or other accommodations, contact the convention center ahead of time at 847-692-2220.
