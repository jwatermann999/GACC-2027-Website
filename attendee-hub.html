Parking & Accessibility

Venue: Donald E. Stephens Convention Center, 5555 N. River Road, Rosemont, IL 60018.

Where to Park

Public parking is available at the William Street Parking Garage (9500 Williams Street) and East Parking Garage (9221 Bryn Mawr Avenue), Rosemont, IL 60018.

Parking rate: Currently $20 for up to 24 hours, with no in-and-out privileges. Rates are set by the venue and may change before the 2027 show.

Getting to the Show

The convention center connects to the William Street garage and nearby hotels via an enclosed skybridge. Follow posted signs to the convention center and show entrance.

Accessible Parking & Entry

Visitors needing accessible parking should follow posted accessible-parking signs at the garages. The convention center has elevator access; follow venue signs for an accessible route to the show floor.

For wheelchair requests or specific accessibility accommodations, please contact the convention center in advance at 847-692-2220 or visit its official visitor information page.

Passenger Drop-Off & Rideshare

Taxi, Uber, Lyft, and passenger drop-off/pickup use the main circular drive entrance at 5555 N. River Road.
