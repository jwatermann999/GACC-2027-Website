Chicago-Area Coin Show, Currency & Collectibles Event

Looking for a coin show near Chicago, an Illinois coin show or a numismatic event in the Midwest? The Great American Coin & Collectibles Show (GACC) takes place at the Donald E. Stephens Convention Center in Rosemont, Illinois. Explore the world of coin collecting, from U.S. and world coins to collectible currency, paper money, gold and silver bullion, precious metals, tokens, exonumia and numismatic collectibles.

Whether you are a longtime numismatist, a new collector researching rare coins or a visitor interested in coin dealers and collectibles, find show details, dealer information, event hours and directions on this site. The 2027 show runs October 27–30, with dealer setup on Wednesday, October 27, and public show days Thursday through Saturday, October 28–30.
