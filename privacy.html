Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 29, 2026

The Great American Coin & Collectibles Show (GACC) respects the information you share with us when visiting our website, requesting dealer space, contacting show management, or subscribing to updates. This notice explains how we use that information and the services involved.

Information you choose to provide

Depending on what you do on this website, we may receive your name, business name, email address, phone number, mailing address, table and badge requests, application details, messages, and email subscription preferences. We use this information to review applications, administer the event, reply to inquiries, and send necessary show-related communications.

Email updates

If you sign up for GACC news and announcements, our email service provider, Brevo, may process your contact information, preferences, and subscription-confirmation information. Marketing email is separate from information needed to answer an inquiry or administer a dealer application. You can unsubscribe from marketing messages using the link provided in those emails.

Dealer applications and other service providers

Our current dealer application submits information through FormSubmit for forwarding to show management. Information you submit is handled by that service as well as by GACC. We also use Google Analytics to understand website usage, which may involve cookies and similar browser technologies. When online payments are introduced, we intend to use Stripe-hosted payment pages or invoices rather than collect full card details through our website. Payment providers will have their own privacy policies.

How information is used and shared

We use information to operate the show, manage applications and communications, maintain the website, and protect our services against spam and abuse. We may share information with service providers working on those purposes and when required by law. We do not publish private dealer application details or subscriber contact lists on the website.

Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for event operations, legitimate business recordkeeping, legal requirements, and related purposes. We apply reasonable safeguards and limit access where practical, but no website or electronic service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and questions

To ask about your information, correct application details, or request deletion where appropriate, email gaccshow@gmail.com. Some records may need to be retained for lawful or business purposes, and third-party service providers may have their own retention obligations.

Updates

We may revise this notice when our website, service providers, or payment features change. The updated notice will appear here with a revised date.
