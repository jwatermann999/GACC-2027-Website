Find Coin Dealers, Currency Specialists & Collectibles

Use the GACC floor map and dealer directory to plan your visit to the Great American Coin & Collectibles Show in Rosemont, near Chicago. The directory is intended to help visitors find participating numismatic dealers and explore specialties such as U.S. coins, world coins, rare coins, collectible paper money, banknotes, gold and silver bullion, tokens, medals, exonumia and other collectibles. Check back for 2027 dealer and floor map updates as show arrangements are finalized.
