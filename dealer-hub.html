Dealer Hub

Everything GACC dealers need before arriving at the Great American Coin & Collectibles Show.

Dealer Load-In Begins: Wednesday, October 27th at 3:00 PM

Dealer Setup & Load-In

Dealer load-in will take place through the Hall G drive-in ramp, located off River Road on the south side of the Donald E. Stephens Convention Center, across from the Crowne Plaza O'Hare. Dealers may drive directly up the ramp and onto the Hall G show floor to unload. Security personnel will be stationed at the entrance and on the show floor to direct vehicles and assist with the load-in process. Once unloaded, please promptly remove your vehicle from the show floor and loading area to keep traffic moving for incoming dealers.

Dealer Security Room

Opens: Tuesday, October 26 at 12:00 PM

Wednesday: Available until 3:00 PM

Security Room Storage Policy

Complimentary storage is included for Table Holders and Equal Access Pass Holders. Show badges must be presented at check-in.

All other participants, including Early Access Pass Holders, are welcome to utilize security room storage for $100 per item.

Late Pickup: $200 per bag after 3:30 PM Wednesday.

Dealer Parking & Accessibility

Venue: Donald E. Stephens Convention Center, 5555 N. River Road, Rosemont, IL 60018.

Parking

Public parking options include the William Street Parking Garage (9500 Williams Street) and East Parking Garage (9221 Bryn Mawr Avenue). Parking rates and garage access are subject to venue policies and may change for 2027.

Dealer Load-In & Load-Out

Dealer unloading is separate from regular visitor parking. Please follow GACC staff directions and posted dealer load-in instructions. Specific 2027 loading arrangements will be confirmed closer to the show. After unloading, promptly move your vehicle to an authorized parking area.

Accessible Parking & Entry

Accessible parking spaces and elevator access are available at the venue. Follow posted signs for accessible routes to the convention center and show floor. For specific accessibility arrangements, contact the convention center at 847-692-2220.

Hotel & Skybridge Access

Enclosed skybridge connections provide access between the convention center and certain nearby hotels and parking facilities. Follow posted signs for the appropriate route.

Security reminder: Parking and unloading arrangements are separate from Dealer Security Room check-in and storage policies.

Dealer Trading Room

The GACC Dealer Trading Room gives participating dealers an opportunity to buy, sell, and trade before the show opens to the public.

Trading Room Access Times

  • 8:00 AM: Trading Room table holders may enter.
  • 8:05 AM: Pass holders may enter, with equal access beginning at this time.
  • 10:15 AM: Early Access admission begins.

Trading Room Tables

Trading Room Table Rules

Maximum of Two W-2 Employees: Only two (2) employees are permitted behind each Trading Room table at any given time. This includes the table owner and employees.

Employees working behind the table must be W-2 employees.

Dealers needing space for additional personnel may purchase additional tables.

Limited Availability: Additional Trading Room tables are available on a first-come, first-served basis, subject to availability.

Important: All participants must have the appropriate credentials for their admission category. Additional Trading Room information will be provided as the show approaches.
